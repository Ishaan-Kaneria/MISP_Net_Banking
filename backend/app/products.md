# MISP Bank policy corpus

## KYC
MISP Bank uses mocked Aadhaar or PAN verification for the demo. Only a mock reference and consent record are retained. Consent is purpose limited and can be withdrawn.

## Grace period
Customers under financial stress can request a 15-day grace period. Credit products are not upsold while a stress flag is active.

## Offers
SIP is suggested after salary credits. Micro-insurance can be suggested after a hospital payment. EMI conversion can help with a large expense. Personal loan offers are never shown as active credit when stress is detected.

## Fraud appeal
A blocked payment is a protective action. The customer can review the contributing amount, time, device, location, and velocity signals and contact support to appeal.
